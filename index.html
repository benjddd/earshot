    How it works
    1. You photograph, upload, paste or drop a picture of a chart.
    2. Claude, Anthropic's model, reads the values in the picture.
    3. You hear a melody of the data, higher values as higher notes, and a spoken headline.
    4. You move through every value in the table by keyboard, each with a note and its spoken value.
    5. Verify checks the reading against your own data, if you have it.

    Questions

    Who is it for?

    It is built for blind and low-vision people who are handed a chart as a picture. It has not yet been tested with screen-reader users.

    Does it store my photo?

    No. Your photo is sent to Claude, Anthropic's model, to be read. This site does not store or log it. A data file you choose for Verify is read in your browser and never leaves the page.

    What charts work?

    Line charts, bar charts, small multiples and heatmaps with readable labels work best. A sharp photo taken straight on, without glare, reads best. Readings can be wrong, so check against the source data when it matters.

    What if a value can't be read?

    It says "Not known" and gives the reason, rather than guess. The same goes for a whole chart that cannot be read.
